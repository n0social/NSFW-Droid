## What to look for (fit)

| Good fit | Poor fit |
| --- | --- |
| “Best NSFW games,” “adult games,” “hentai games,” “visual novel” catalogs | Tube/streaming/cam-only lists |
| Sites that link out to **Steam / itch / Nutaku** etc. | Sites that expect you to host video or APKs |
| Lists that label **affiliate** or sponsored rows | Sites that forbid affiliate links without disclosure |
| Reviewers who understand **18+ game** niche | Lists that only rank “free porn sites” |

**Name people mention:** [The Porn Dude](https://theporndude.com) and similar **review aggregators** — only worth pitching if they maintain a **games** (or equivalent) section. Many clones exist; prefer lists that already show **game catalogs** or **itch/Steam** style entries.

**Also consider:** niche blogs/roundups, “top adult games” articles that accept suggestions, and game-focused NSFW communities that maintain a **wiki or pinned resource list** (read their rules before asking).

## Example outreach email

Use when a directory **already lists NSFW games or game catalogs** and you want to suggest **NSFW Droid**. Customize the bracketed parts; keep the factual claims accurate.

**Subject:** Suggestion: NSFW Droid — 18+ game catalog (link-out, not a tube site)

```text
Hello [Editor name or “team”],

I’m writing to suggest NSFW Droid for your [games / adult games / hentai games] section, if you maintain a list of NSFW game catalogs.

What we are:
• https://www.nsfwdroid.com — a curated 18+ game catalog (mobile & web) that links players to official store pages (itch.io, Steam, and similar). We do not host video or game binaries.
• Age verification on entry; affiliate relationships are disclosed on-site: https://www.nsfwdroid.com/affiliate
• Public game pages for discovery and SEO, e.g. [https://www.nsfwdroid.com/game/ONE-LIVE-SLUG]

What we are not:
• Not a tube, cam, or creator platform — only a game listing site with outbound links.

If a catalog like ours fits your criteria, we’d appreciate being considered for a listing or review. We also publish curated outbound links to other game-focused hubs (not tube lists) at https://www.nsfwdroid.com/links — happy to add vetted directories there when appropriate.

I’m happy to answer questions about content policy, how listings are sourced, or to provide a different example URL.

Thank you for the work you do curating [niche] resources.

Best regards,
[Your name]
NSFW Droid
https://www.nsfwdroid.com
[Optional: contact email — use a stable address you check, e.g. newsletter@ or ops@ on nsfwdroid.com]
```

**Tips:**

- Send from a **real** address on `nsfwdroid.com` if possible (matches brand).  
- One short paragraph beats a marketing deck.  
- If they sell **paid placement**, decide separately; read terms and affiliate rules before paying.  
- Do not misrepresent traffic or “#1” claims.
